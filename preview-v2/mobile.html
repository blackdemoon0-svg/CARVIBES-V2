Rules

How mobile behaves

Thumb-first targets

Every tap target is at least 44 px; the assistant's choices sit in the lower half of the screen.

Swipe, not squeeze

Card lists become horizontal snap rails so cars stay big instead of shrinking into a cramped grid.

No horizontal scroll

Overflow is clipped at the layout level; long words and wide tables reflow instead of pushing the page.

Light animation

Particles and heavy blur are desktop-only; mobile keeps fast fades and 60 fps transforms.

Safe areas honoured

Sticky bars and floating controls respect notches and the home indicator (env(safe-area-inset)).

All 10 languages

Longer translations (French, German) and RTL (Arabic) reflow without breaking any component.
