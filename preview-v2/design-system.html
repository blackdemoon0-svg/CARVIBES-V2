V2 identity

Design system

The tokens behind the new CarVibes: near-black surfaces, electric blue and cyan as the light source, violet and magenta used sparingly as accents, and warm off-white type. Displays are Archivo uppercase at tight tracking; UI copy is Inter.

06 — Motion & depth

Fast, subtle, never decorative

Hover lift

Reveal

fade + 20 px slide-up once, on scroll into view · 700 ms

Parallax

Radial halos + masked grid + film grain · fixed, one layer each

Particles

34 drifting points, desktop only, paused when the tab is hidden

08 — Accessibility

Built in, not bolted on

Contrast

Body copy sits at ≥ 4.6:1 on the near-black surfaces; cyan is used for light, not for paragraphs.

Keyboard

Skip link, visible focus rings, Escape closes every overlay, tap targets ≥ 44 px.

Semantics

One H1 per screen, landmarks (header/nav/main/footer), aria-pressed on toggles, alt text on every car.
